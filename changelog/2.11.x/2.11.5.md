# 2.11.5 — 2026-11-27

## Changed

- **`@cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` `^0.13.22` → `^0.13.24`**: a service error of a declared reason carries `data.retryable`, and `content[]` shows a `retryable` / `not retryable` line.
- **Dev dependencies**: `@biomejs/biome` ^2.6.8 → ^2.6.9, `@types/node` 26.6.6 → 26.6.11, `@vitest/coverage-istanbul` and `vitest` ^5.1.1 → ^5.1.2, `fast-check` ^4.10.3 → ^4.11.0, `ignore` ^7.0.16 → ^7.0.17, `vite` ^8.3.8 → ^8.4.0, `tsc-alias` ^1.9.10 → ^1.9.11.
- **`skills/clinicaltrialsgov-guide/SKILL.md`** moves to version 1.2: `DISTANCE[]` bounds, `RANGE[]` inside `SEARCH[Location](…)`, the radius cap, and the added in-country site.

## Fixed

- **`clinicaltrials_get_study_results`** re-sends the batch without an NCT ID that ClinicalTrials.gov refuses as malformed, instead of fetching every ID one by one; `fetchErrors` names it, and `clinicaltrials_search_studies` `ids_not_found` names the refused ID in `data.invalid` ([#207](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/207)).
- **A 400 that names no cause and only repeats the expression as sent** reads as `field_invalid` when the expression holds `SEARCH[Location]`, and as `query_parse_error` with its own hint otherwise; refusal words inside a caller's quoted phrase or an upstream token quote are no longer read as a refused field ([#208](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/208)).
- **`clinicaltrials_find_eligible`** under `location.near` adds one recruiting site in `location.country` when none of a candidate's listed sites is there ([#210](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/210)).
- **A `location.near.radiusMi` or `geoFilter` radius past 12,451 mi (20,039 km)** is sent at that cap, where a radius past about 1.1e305 mi drew a bare upstream 400; the echo shows the radius sent, the notice says so, and at the cap the no-match notice gives the replay step instead of "widen" ([#211](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/211)).
- **A `DISTANCE[]` radius past 500 mi or 805 km** reads as an unreadable radius, with those bounds and a pointer to `geoFilter` / `location.near`, where it read as a generic syntax error.
- **A `RANGE[]` term inside `SEARCH[Location](…)`** gets levers that work: a radius beside the wrapper, or the site field matched by value OR-joined inside it. It used to be told to move the term beside the wrapper, where no site field but `NumLocationContacts` takes `RANGE[]`.
- **A `geoFilter` number in exponent notation** gets the plain-decimal-digits message, where it got the bare-radius one.
