# 2.11.4 — 2026-11-21

## Added

- **`clinicaltrials_find_eligible` `location.near`** (`lat`, `lon`, `radiusMi`) matches by distance from the patient: each candidate returns its sites within the radius, nearest first, with `distanceMi`, and the `Sites:` line follows. The input is echoed as `near`, the upstream filter as `searchCriteria.geoFilter`, and `near` beside `city` or `state` fails as the new `location_conflict` reason ([#205](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/205)).
- **`clinicaltrials_find_eligible`** returns each site's own `contacts[]`; central contacts gain `role` and `phoneExt` ([#202](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/202)).
- **`clinicaltrials_get_study_results` summary mode** carries per-group adverse-event totals in `eventGroups` and the participant-flow group roster with every period's milestones ([#203](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/203)).
- **`clinicaltrials_get_study_results`** returns `resultPublications` (RESULT references with `pmid`, `citation`, and `retractions`) on entries for studies without posted results ([#204](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/204)).

## Changed

- **`clinicaltrials_get_study_results`** per-ID fallback records only `study_not_found`; any other per-ID failure ends the call with its own typed error ([#201](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/201)).
- **`skills/clinicaltrialsgov-guide/SKILL.md`** moves to version 1.1: it covers `DISTANCE[]`, `location.near`, and `resultPublications`.

## Fixed

- **`clinicaltrials_search_studies`** caps a page at 75 studies, or the server's `CT_MAX_PAGE_SIZE` when lower, when `fields` names `ProtocolSection`; `truncated`, `shown`, `cap`, and the notice disclose it ([#199](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/199)).
- **A `DISTANCE[]` or `RANGE[]` term inside `SEARCH[Location](…)`** that ClinicalTrials.gov refuses with its Java exception sentence is `field_invalid` with a hint of its own, naming each radius input and the tool that has it ([#206](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/206)).
- **The note on an added recruiting site** in `clinicaltrials_find_eligible` names the home-country rule; it said "the nearest recruiting site" ([#205](https://github.com/cyanheads/clinicaltrialsgov-mcp-server/issues/205)).
- **A contact with an extension and no phone** no longer prints `undefined ext. N`, in `clinicaltrials_get_study_record` contacts and the point of contact in `clinicaltrials_get_study_results`.
- **Count labels** in `clinicaltrials_get_study_results` and `clinicaltrials_get_study_record`'s `## Results Summary` agree in number at 1.
- **The `geoFilter` rejection of a radius with no unit** says what such a radius matches: only sites geocoded to the center point.
- **Three further `DISTANCE[]` refusals** get their own readings: on a field that is not a point (`field_invalid`), with an operand ClinicalTrials.gov cannot read (`query_parse_error`), and with no `AREA[…]` before it.
